WhatsApp Voice Note Transcriber

ElevenLabs Scribe v2 · runs only on web.whatsapp.com

How it reads the audio. WhatsApp keeps voice notes out of the page until you press play, so the extension triggers its download and intercepts the audio at the moment playback would start — the real play() is never called, so nothing is audible. WhatsApp may still register the note as played on its side (blue mic for the sender); that part is out of the extension's hands.
Separate speakers
Useful for forwarded recordings with more than one voice.
